WHAT THE SOFTWARE SENDS, AND WHEN The Software contacts the internet only for licensing, and only: - when you activate a licence; - when you release a computer; - at most once every 30 days, in the background, to revalidate - and only if a connection happens to be available. What is sent on those occasions: - your licence key; - an activation label of the form "Standee Maker" followed by eight hexadecimal characters. Those characters are part of a one-way hash derived from a Windows installation identifier. They let you tell your activations apart in your licence portal so you know which computer to release. The original identifier never leaves your computer, and the hash cannot be turned back into it. Nothing else is sent. Not your images, not your file names, not your settings, not your IP address beyond what any internet request unavoidably reveals to the receiving server. Legal basis: performance of the contract between you and us (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Verifying a licence is how we provide what you bought.
