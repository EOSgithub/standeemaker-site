Questions

Frequently asked

What can I do with the trial?
Everything. I decided to hold nothing back in the trial: the files come out at full size and full quality, with no watermark. The only limits are how many and how long: one new subject every seven days, and fourteen days after the first run the program stops writing files. If all you mean to print is one stand for your favourite card, you don’t need anything else.
Do I need an internet connection?
Never to work: images, SVGs and STLs do not leave your computer. It is needed once, to activate your licence key; the other 99% of the time Standee Maker runs offline.
How many computers can I install it on?
Two, owned or controlled by you. A licence can move: you release the activation on one machine and activate it on another.
What printer do I need?
The one you have. Standee Maker writes STL files: you open them in your slicer like any other model. The starting values are meant for an FDM printer with a 0.4 mm nozzle, and that is where the minimum line width (0.8 mm) makes sense.
Which images can I use?
Yours, or ones you have the right to use. The program does not check and does not judge: it converts what you give it, and the subject stays your responsibility.
What if it turns out not to be for me?
The trial is there to find that out before you pay. After a purchase the Terms of sale apply.
