What is the Beta?
The full program, free, before the paid version exists. Files come out at full size and full quality, with no watermark, and there is no limit on subjects or exports. It may have rough edges and it will change: tell us what works and what does not. A Beta copy stops being a Beta after 90 days of use, or sooner if we announce that the Beta is over. Version 1.0 installs over it and keeps your library.
Do I need an internet connection?
Not to use it. The Beta needs no key and no sign-in. Once a day at most it asks this site whether a newer version exists or the Beta has ended, and you can switch that off in Settings. Images, SVGs and STLs never leave your computer.
How many computers can I install it on?
As many of your own as you like during the Beta. The paid licence will cover two computers you own or control, and it will be movable.
What printer do I need?
The one you have. Standee Maker writes STL files that open in your slicer like any other model. The defaults suit an FDM printer with a 0.4 mm nozzle, which is where the minimum line width of 0.8 mm comes from.
Which images can I use?
Your own, or ones you have the right to use. The program does not check what you give it, so the subject is your responsibility. Neither the Beta nor a licence gives you any rights to someone else's artwork.
Which pictures give the best results?
The cleaner the lines, the less there is to fix. Best of all is line art: black lines on white or on a transparent background, like a colouring page. Switch on Line Art Mode and it traces them as they are. Next come PNGs with a transparent background, such as character art: the outline is already there. Then drawings and renders with clear edges on a plain background, which Remove background clears in a few clicks. Photos, soft shading and busy backgrounds work too, but expect more time in Touch up.
Can I sell what I print?
Not at the moment. The Beta and the launch licence are for personal use: print for yourself and give away what you print. A licence for selling is not on offer yet, and whether it will be depends on who asks: write to us if you need one. Either way you may not sell or share the STL and SVG files themselves, nor sell objects made from the sample drawings that come with the program.
Windows says it protected my PC. What now?
That is SmartScreen. Windows shows it for downloads it has not seen many times yet, and every new release of a small program starts there. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Download the setup only from this page.
Does this page track me?
During the Beta it counts visits with GoatCounter, to see how many people look at the project and how many download it. No cookies, nothing stored on your computer, and GoatCounter does not keep your IP address. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the counter is not loaded at all. The details are in the privacy notice.
What happens when the Beta ends?
If the Beta goes well, the paid version comes out at €19.99, once, with no subscription, and we announce it here and in the program. People on the launch list get 50% off during its first month. Beta copies stop being a Beta, and you install 1.0 over them without losing your library. What you made during the Beta is yours for personal use and stays usable. The Terms of sale apply only from then.
